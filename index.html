About

Professional profile

Shefali Jarial is an advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Punjab & Haryana, practising in civil and criminal law.

She practises before the Punjab & Haryana High Court at Chandigarh and is a member of the Punjab & Haryana High Court Bar Association. Her work covers civil suits and appeals, bail and quashing petitions, criminal trials, family and property matters, and drafting of notices, agreements and replies.
